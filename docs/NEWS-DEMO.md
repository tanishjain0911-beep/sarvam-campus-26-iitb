# Suno News Mirror — 45-second demo pitch

## Pitch (45s)

1. 0-8s: "Suno News Mirror reads today's headlines aloud."
2. 8-18s: Pick a topic; see current RSS headlines with source and time.
3. 18-30s: Choose a listening language; Suno translates and speaks in a standard voice.
4. 30-40s: Optional beta: upload a consented own-voice WAV to mirror your voice.
5. 40-45s: Listen to the audio; core flow needs no account.

## What it shows

- Topic-selected current RSS headlines with source and publication time.
- Translation into a chosen listening language.
- Standard text-to-speech voice output.
- Optional, consented own-voice beta WAV upload (beta).

## Flow

- Choose topic -> review headlines (source, time) -> pick listening language -> Generate -> play audio.
- Own-voice beta: explicit consent -> upload WAV -> use for that session.

## Honest limitations

- Feed refresh is not guaranteed to publish within a minute; availability depends on source feeds.
- Voice clone quality varies; no guarantee of exact accent or tone.
- No accounts, paid hosting, or full-article scraping; headline snippets only.
- Beta feature; may be unavailable or change.
- No benchmark, accuracy, or uniqueness claims are made.
